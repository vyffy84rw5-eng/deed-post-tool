Schedule and publish your gym's short videos to TikTok.

DEED Post Tool is the publishing tool used by Personal Gym DEED (Shinjuku, Tokyo) to post its own training and workout videos to its TikTok account at planned times.

パーソナルジムDEED（東京都新宿区）が、自ジムで制作したトレーニング動画を、自ジムのTikTokアカウントへ予定どおりに投稿するための道具です。

Features

Posting queue

Videos made by the gym are lined up with a caption and a planned date and time.

Review before posting

Staff see the video, caption and privacy setting on one screen and confirm before anything is sent to TikTok.

Official TikTok APIs

Uses TikTok Login Kit for sign-in and the Content Posting API (Direct Post) to publish. No passwords are stored.

Status check

After posting, the tool shows whether TikTok finished processing the video.

One account only

The tool is used only with the gym's own TikTok account (@deed_gym).

Disconnect anytime

Access can be revoked from TikTok settings. Stored tokens are then deleted.

How it works

  1. Connect. A gym staff member signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves the requested permissions: basic profile, video upload and video publish.
  2. Prepare. The gym's content team adds a finished video (MP4, vertical 9:16) and its caption to the posting queue.
  3. Review. On the posting screen, staff check the video preview, caption, privacy level and interaction settings, then press Post.
  4. Publish. The tool sends the video to TikTok through the Content Posting API and shows the result.

使い方：①担当者がTikTokでログインして許可 ②完成した動画と説明文を並べる ③投稿画面で中身と公開設定を確かめて「投稿」 ④TikTokの公式の仕組みで投稿され、結果が表示されます。

Data we use

WhatTikTok open_id, display name, avatar, access token, privacy options for posting, and basic post status.
WhyOnly to publish the gym's own videos and to check that each post succeeded.
SharingNever sold or shared with third parties, except where required by law.
DeletionWhen the TikTok connection is removed, stored data for that account is deleted.
